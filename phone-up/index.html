Phone Up! Charades

A guessing game for your crew. Choose a deck, hold the phone at your forehead with the screen facing your friends, and guess before time runs out.

How to play

Tilt down for Correct and up for Pass. Return through the starting position after each answer. Touch the left side to pass or the right side for a correct answer. Keep a secure grip and use gentle movements.

Everyday Objects, Basic Actions and custom decks are free. An optional rewarded video unlocks the selected premium deck for 30 minutes. Phone Up! Pro opens all decks and removes ads while your subscription is active. Prices and trial eligibility are shown in the app.
