Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026. This policy describes Nura and this website.

Who is responsible?

Nura is operated by Tuğçe Özkahya, Türkiye. For privacy questions and requests, contact destek.ydscalisma@gmail.com.

Your journal and local preferences

Journal notes, saved passages, reading and program progress, highlights, reminder settings and app preferences are stored on your device. Local journal notes are not separately encrypted by Nura. Operating-system backups may include app data. Core features do not require an account.

Menstrual and postpartum flow choices are optional manual preferences stored locally. Nura does not record a cycle calendar or predict fertility. These choices are excluded from Nura's manual backup and account-sync snapshot. Nura does not send journal contents, religious-practice records, these flow choices or precise prayer coordinates to advertisers.

Location and notifications

You can select a city without granting location access. If you choose “Use my location,” foreground location is used for local prayer-time calculations. Coordinates stay in local preferences and are excluded from Nura backup/sync; Nura does not upload them. No background location is requested. Optional reminders use your device's notification permission and local scheduling.

Advertising and privacy choices

Nura uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for native, interstitial and optional rewarded advertising. Ad requests depend on UMP eligibility and any applicable privacy choices. The app requests non-personalized ads and applies restricted ad-content settings. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location derived from IP), device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, diagnostic information and performance data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain its services. Your private journal and precise prayer location are not supplied as ad-request targeting data. Google and applicable ad providers process data under their own retention and international-transfer policies. See Google's Privacy Policy, Google's SDK data disclosure and Google ad controls.

Where a privacy form is available, use the app's advertising privacy options to review your choices. Device privacy settings also control permissions and available advertising identifiers. Refusing optional consent does not prevent core reading or journaling. A completed rewarded ad stores only an ad-free expiry time locally for the 30-minute reward.

Audio, speech and external services

Streaming or downloading a recitation connects to MP3Quran's audio hosting. Like any internet request, this exposes technical information such as your IP address and the requested audio file to the provider. Previously downloaded audio can be played offline. See MP3Quran's policy. Editorial text narration uses your device's speech voices; network processing depends on your operating system and selected voice. Nura does not send this narration to a Nura-operated AI voice service.

Backups, optional accounts and purchases

Manual backup files are encrypted before you choose where to save or share them. Your chosen storage provider's policy applies. Keep your recovery phrase safely; we cannot recover it. Nura does not automatically upload your journal to iCloud or Google Drive.

If an optional account service is configured and offered in your app, it uses Supabase for email authentication and an encrypted snapshot uploaded only when you choose Upload. The recovery phrase is not sent. Account deletion in the account screen removes the account and linked snapshot; local notes and copies you saved elsewhere must be deleted separately. See Supabase's policy. Account sync is unavailable when the service is not configured.

If store purchase services are enabled in your app, Apple or Google processes payment details, and RevenueCat processes app-user identifiers, purchases and entitlements. We do not receive payment-card numbers. See RevenueCat's policy. No subscription purchase is promised by this website; available products and prices must appear in your app's store purchase screen.

Support, retention and your rights

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include through Gmail. We use it to respond to support or privacy requests, maintain security and meet legal obligations. Do not send journal entries, passwords, recovery phrases or payment-card details.

Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app; device backups and exported copies may remain separately. We retain support correspondence only as needed for the request, follow-up, security and legal obligations, then delete it. Third-party services control their own logs and retention. No fixed third-party deletion period is promised.

Where applicable, processing is based on providing requested services, consent for optional processing, legitimate interests in support/security, or legal obligations. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion or portability, object or restrict processing, withdraw consent and complain to your data-protection authority. Email destek.ydscalisma@gmail.com; we may need proportionate identity verification. We cannot access or remotely erase notes kept only on your device. Services may process data outside your country under their applicable transfer arrangements.

Young users and this website

Nura is intended for teens and adults, not as a service specifically directed to young children. If you believe a child supplied personal information to us, contact us to request its deletion.

This website has no advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. External links have their own policies. We will update this page and its date when our practices change.

Günlüğünüz ve yerel tercihler

Regl ve lohusalık akışları isteğe bağlı, elle seçilen yerel tercihlerdir. Nura döngü takvimi tutmaz, doğurganlık tahmini yapmaz. Bu tercihler Nura'nın elle oluşturulan yedeklerine ve hesap senkronizasyonuna alınmaz. Günlük içerikleri, dinî pratik kayıtları, özel gün tercihleri veya kesin namaz konumu reklamverenlere gönderilmez.

Konum ve bildirimler

Konum izni vermeden şehir seçebilirsiniz. “Konumumu kullan” seçeneğinde ön plan konumu cihazda namaz vakitleri hesaplamak için kullanılır. Koordinatlar yerel tercihlerde kalır, Nura yedek/senkronizasyonuna alınmaz ve Nura tarafından yüklenmez. Arka plan konumu istenmez. İsteğe bağlı hatırlatmalar cihazın bildirim izniyle yerel olarak planlanır.
