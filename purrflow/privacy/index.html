Privacy Policy

Purr Flow · Updated: 24 October 2026

Who we are

Purr Flow is developed by Tuğçe Özkahya. Contact: destek.ydscalisma@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Focus history, session durations, streaks, preferences, sound mixes, your cat’s name and appearance, and decorations are stored locally. We do not upload this focus history or cat customization to our own server. Device backups may include local data according to your device settings.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and provide Plus access. It processes an app user identifier, transaction and subscription information, and technical information such as device platform and app version. Network services may receive your IP address. Apple or Google handles payment; we do not receive your full payment card details. See RevenueCat’s privacy information.

Support and purposes

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this to respond and resolve issues. Subscription data is used to deliver and restore paid access, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. Where applicable, processing is based on performance of a contract, legal obligations, and legitimate interests in support and service security.

Sharing, retention and international processing

Service providers including RevenueCat, Google AdMob and the app stores process the data needed for their services. Processing may occur outside your country, including in the United States. We keep support correspondence only as needed to resolve requests and meet legal obligations. Subscription records may be retained for service operation, dispute resolution and legal obligations. See also Google’s policy linked in the advertising section.

Your choices and rights

You can remove local app data through your device’s app storage controls or by deleting the app; backup copies are managed separately in device/cloud settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of data we process. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection and a complaint to a data protection authority. We may need information to locate your subscription record. Please never send passwords or card details.

Optional rewarded advertising and Google AdMob

Versions with rewarded advertising use Google AdMob to offer temporary access to selected features when you choose to watch an advertisement. Free features remain available without watching ads. Plus subscribers are not offered rewarded ads. Earlier versions without the advertising SDK do not use AdMob.

Google and its service providers may process IP addresses (including to estimate general location), device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash logs and performance data to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and operate and improve their services. This processing is not limited to information you enter into the app. Ad requests in the current implementation are configured as non-personalized; this does not mean that no personal data is processed.

Where required, we use Google's User Messaging Platform to request privacy choices before ads are requested. You can decline consent and, where available, review or change your choices through Ad privacy options in the app's settings. Consent-based processing relies on your consent where required, which you may withdraw for future processing. Choosing not to watch an ad does not remove access to free features. Temporary rewards and daily reward counts are stored on your device. Google's retention and international processing practices are described in its Privacy Policy.

Children and updates

The app does not require an account or ask for your age. Children should use purchases with a parent or guardian’s permission. Contact us if a child has sent personal information through support. If data practices change, this page and its date will be updated.

Onayı reddedebilir, sunulduğu yerlerde uygulama ayarlarındaki Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri üzerinden tercihlerini inceleyebilir veya değiştirebilirsin.
