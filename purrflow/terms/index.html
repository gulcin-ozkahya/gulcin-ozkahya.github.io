About Purr Flow

Purr Flow provides focus timers, ambient audio and a customizable cat companion. It is a productivity and relaxation tool, not medical treatment or a guarantee of sleep or productivity. Developer: Tuğçe Özkahya.

Free features and Plus

Free features include the 25-minute focus timer, the white cat, Rain or Wind with one sound at a time, basic statistics, and session-earned cushion and mug decorations. Plus adds longer focus durations, unlimited sleep, additional cat coats, all available sounds with up to three mixed tracks, weekly history and premium decorations. Some decorations also require completed focus sessions.

Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Access normally continues until the paid period ends, subject to store adjustments or refunds.
